Six literature-derived article images were present in the authoritative local
source archive. They are not original experimental data and are not required
by the verified rebuild. They have been omitted from this distribution
candidate because redistribution permission was not established for every
image. The immutable source manifest retains their filenames and SHA-256
values, while citations and derived audit records remain in the package.

Omitted files: `R032_PMC5247568_Fig4_gr4.jpg`,
`R033_PMC6958642_Fig2.jpg`, `R039_PMC3859859_Fig2.jpg`,
`R045_PMC5667543_Fig2.jpg`, `R060_PMC4454361_Fig1.jpg`, and

repository owner documents an applicable redistribution license or permission.
